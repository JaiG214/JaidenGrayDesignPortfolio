Design Philosophy & Vision

I developed the original concept for Fro Bros and established hair and hair culture as the foundation of its design philosophy. This framework guided the creation of our gameplay systems and helped maintain a cohesive vision across disciplines.

Combat & Systems Design

As Lead Designer, I helped design the game's core combat systems, including hairstyle abilities, weapons, accessories, enemies, and progression. I also created design documentation and worked with artists and programmers to translate these mechanics into gameplay.

Production & Leadership

Alongside my design responsibilities, I took on half of the team's production work, helping coordinate our 11-person team. I organized tasks, tracked progress, facilitated communication between disciplines, and developed new workflows to improve sprint planning and execution.

CORE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Hair became the foundation of the game

Fro Bros began with my idea to build an action game centered around hair and its cultural significance. I wanted hair to influence more than the game's visual identity; it needed to shape how players fought, built their characters, and interacted with the world.

That philosophy became the foundation of our core systems:

  • Hairstyles became distinct ability sets and playstyles.
  • Hair tools and products became weapons and accessories.
  • Ways to damage your hair inspired enemies and dungeon environments.
  • Design consistency: I ensured that every new mechanic and piece of content connected back to the game's central theme of hair.

GAMEPLAY SYSTEMS DESIGN

Turning hair into gameplay

Once I made hair the foundation of Fro Bros, I looked for ways to translate different parts of hair culture into gameplay systems.

Every hairstyle, a different playstyle

Hairstyles act as the player's ability kits, with each one designed around a different approach to combat.

  • Afro: The starting hairstyle, offering a versatile balance of offensive and defensive abilities that help players learn the game's core combat.
  • Waves: A reactive, mobility-focused playstyle that rewards players for understanding enemy attacks, avoiding danger, and knowing when to respond.
  • Bantu Knots: An offense-focused playstyle that emphasizes aggression, allowing players to fight through danger and aggressively control fights.
Hairstyle gameplay media

Hair tools reimagined as weapons

Weapons were designed around tools used to style and care for hair, including combs, brushes, blow dryers, and more. I explored how these familiar objects could be reimagined as weapons, giving each one its own combat mechanics and behaviors.

  • Hot Comb: A flaming sword that burns enemies with each swing and can spread fire between enemies.
  • Blow Dryer: A ranged weapon with three interchangeable attachments, each firing air in a different way.
  • Razor Combs: A pair of throwable, boomerang-like weapons that cause enemies to bleed, with repeated hits increasing the damage.
Weapon gameplay media

Hair products shape your build

Hair products/accessories found on a dungeon run provide passive buffs and modify the player's build.

  • Beads of Prey: Three winged hair beads orbit the player, intercept incoming projectiles, and activate different effects depending on their color.
  • Weave: A package of hair extensions that rewards players for weaving through enemy attacks, increasing their chance to dodge damage whenever they narrowly avoid getting hit.

Hairpins, on the other hand, are accessories that players can choose to equip before a dungeon run.

  • Heart Hairpin: Grants the player one additional maximum heart and clearing a floor restores one heart.
Accessory gameplay media

Hair damage shaped the dungeon

Each dungeon area was designed around a different way people can damage their hair, allowing the game's central theme to shape both the environment and its hazards.

  • Conditioner Commons: Represents product buildup, with spilled hair products, clutter, and messy environments reflecting excessive product use.
  • Frizz Factory: Represents heat damage through an industrial factory filled with heat-styling machinery, electricity, and hazardous equipment.
Dungeon design media

Hair culture shaped the enemy roster

Enemies were designed more loosely around different aspects of hair and hair culture. Some were inspired by hair products, while others represented ways someone could damage their hair.

  • Bleach Blob: Inspired by the thick, creamy appearance of purple-tinted hair lightener. Its poisonous trail causes poison buildup on the player, reflecting the damaging effects of bleaching hair.

Sometimes, we designed enemies around fun hair-related puns. When I shared my ideas for Conditioner Commons with our concept artist, she incorporated a Greek bathhouse theme into the environment. This inspired me to create the Mohawklite enemy.

  • Mohawklite: Inspired by ancient Greek hoplite soldiers, with an emphasis on their helmets' distinctive mohawk-like crests.

DESIGNING DEFENSIVE OPTIONS

Every hairstyle had its own way to defend

Because Fro Bros incorporates bullet-hell combat, dealing with incoming projectiles is something every hairstyle has to account for. I saw this as an opportunity to differentiate each hairstyle's playstyle by giving players a different way to deal with the bullets around them.

  • Afro: Stands its ground, reflecting or absorbing incoming projectiles while rewarding precisely timed defenses.
  • Waves: Stays mobile, using invincibility frames to move through projectiles and interrupts to stop enemy attacks before they happen.
  • Bantu Knots: Fights through danger, destroying incoming projectiles with its offensive abilities.
Afro defensive gameplay clip

AFRO

Standing Your Ground

  • Frotection: Puts the player into a stationary defensive stance, using their Afro as a shield to reflect incoming projectiles back at enemies. Perfectly timed defenses reduce the ability's cooldown, providing an additional layer of mastery as players improve their timing when reflecting incoming projectiles.
  • Frosorption: Absorbs incoming projectiles and stores their power. The player can release the stored blast the next time they activate the ability, allowing them to save that power for a more advantageous moment in combat.
Waves defensive gameplay clip

WAVES

Movement & Interrupts

  • Wave Dash: Gives the player invincibility frames, allowing them to move directly through incoming projectiles and reposition during fights.
  • Movement Speed: One of Waves' abilities provides a temporary movement speed bonus, allowing players to maneuver around enemies and avoid incoming attacks more effectively.
  • Attack Interrupts: Three of Waves' abilities can interrupt enemies, rewarding players for recognizing when an enemy is about to attack or is already performing an attack animation and responding at the right moment.
Bantu Knots defensive gameplay clip

BANTU KNOTS

Destroying Projectiles

  • Offensive Defense: Two of Bantu Knots' abilities can destroy incoming enemy projectiles, allowing players to clear bullets and create openings by attacking directly into incoming fire, reinforcing the hairstyle's aggressive ranged playstyle.

DESIGNING WEAPONS

Making Weapons Exciting

During development, we identified a design issue: once players found a weapon they liked, they had little reason to try other weapons, making future weapon chest rewards less exciting.

To address this, we introduced a weapon upgrade system:

  • Duplicate Weapons: Finding a weapon the player already owns upgrades it instead.
  • Meaningful Upgrades: Rather than simply increasing damage, upgrades introduce new mechanics or expand existing ones.
  • Rewarding Discovery: Weapon upgrades keep duplicate discoveries exciting while giving players new reasons to experiment with different weapons.
Hot Comb Gameplay Clip

Hot Comb

The Hot Comb's defining mechanic is its ability to apply Heat damage, a DoT effect that burns enemies. Rather than simply increasing its damage, I designed its upgrades around what fire naturally does: spread.

  • Tier 2: Heat damage lasts longer and spreads between enemies that get close to one another.
  • Tier 3: Burning enemies release a heatwave upon death, spreading Heat damage to nearby enemies. The Hot Comb also gains attack speed for each burning enemy.

These upgrades encourage players to spread Heat damage to as many enemies as possible, rewarding them with increased attack speed rather than just additional DoT.

Blow Dryer Gameplay Clip

Blow Dryer

The Blow Dryer has three attachments, each with a different attack. Players are forced to cycle between them whenever they stop firing, with a brief period where they cannot attack while switching.

  • Tier 2: Releasing the attack button triggers a brief timing minigame where pressing right-click at the right moment allows players to switch attachments faster.
  • Tier 3: Switching attachments temporarily augments their attacks, giving each attachment a unique initial boost.

These upgrades are designed around the downtime caused by switching attachments. Tier 2 rewards precise timing to reduce that downtime, while Tier 3 rewards players for timing their attacks after switching to take full advantage of each attachment's initial boost.

Razor Combs Gameplay Clip

Razor Combs

The Razor Combs are a pair of boomerang-like weapons that apply Nicked, a stacking DoT effect that bleeds enemies. When thrown, each comb can hit enemies on its outgoing path, but only one enemy on its return path before bouncing to the ground near the player, where it can be picked up.

  • Tier 2: All hits apply Nicked, with returning hits applying 2 stacks instead of 1.
  • Tier 3: Catching a Razor Comb immediately after it lands applies an additional stack of Nicked to every enemy it previously hit. Reaching 5 stacks causes Nicked to burst, dealing its remaining damage instantly and removing the effect.

These upgrades encourage players to position their throws and time their catches to build Nicked stacks as quickly as possible. Reaching the maximum of 5 stacks rewards players with a burst of damage, making precise positioning and timing essential to getting the most out of the weapon.

DESIGNING BUILD VARIETY

Encouraging Player Experimentation

I wanted accessories to do more than simply improve the player's stats. They should encourage players to approach combat differently and experiment with combinations of mechanics.

  • Changing Player Behavior: Rewarding players for making different decisions during combat.
  • Introducing Risk & Reward: Offering stronger effects alongside meaningful trade-offs.
  • Creating Synergies Between Systems: Encouraging players to combine weapons, hairstyle abilities, and other mechanics.

DESIGNING ENEMY INTERACTIONS

Creating Interesting Combat Encounters

I wanted enemies to create interesting combat situations through their individual behaviors and how they interact with one another.

  • Enemy Synergies: Combining different enemy behaviors to create meaningful decisions about positioning, risk, and which threats to prioritize.
  • More to Come: Additional enemy designs and interactions will be explored as development progresses.
Creating Enemy Synergies Gameplay Clip

Creating Enemy Synergies

I designed enemies whose behaviors create interesting decisions when combined, encouraging players to prioritize threats and weigh different risks.

  • Bleach Blob: Leaves poisonous trails that restrict safe movement, with poison gradually building up before dealing damage.
  • Spritzer: Retreats when approached, but when given space, fires projectiles that don't directly target the player, creating bullet patterns players must navigate.

Together, these enemies give players different ways to approach an encounter, each with its own risks:

  • Play Cat and Mouse: Chase the Spritzer to prevent it from attacking while focusing fire on Bleach Blobs, but risk crossing their poisonous trails.
  • Eliminate the Spritzer: Focus on killing the Spritzer early, but give Bleach Blobs more time to spread poison throughout the room.
  • Eliminate the Bleach Blobs: Focus on removing the source of poisonous terrain, but give the Spritzer opportunities to fill the room with projectiles.
